Microgrid kits

Hamlet kit (10 kW)

The Hamlet kit powers up to 60 homes with lighting, fans and phone charging, and includes 30 kWh of lithium iron phosphate storage.

Village kit (40 kW)

The Village kit serves up to 250 homes and small businesses such as flour mills and tailoring shops, with smart meters on every connection.

Clinic kit (15 kW)

The Clinic kit keeps vaccine refrigerators, lights and delivery-room equipment running through the night with a dedicated priority circuit.
