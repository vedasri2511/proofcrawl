Why microgrids beat diesel generators

Diesel generators remain the default backup in many villages, but fuel costs and maintenance make them expensive to run for more than a few hours a day. A solar microgrid with storage has a higher upfront cost but its running cost is close to zero once installed.

Across our sites, the levelised cost of electricity from microgrids is roughly forty percent lower than diesel over a ten-year period.
