About the cooperative

Verdant Grid Cooperative was founded in 2019 by a group of electrical engineers and farmer-producer organisations in Hyderabad. The cooperative is owned by its 5,400 member households, and every member gets one vote at the annual general meeting.

Our mission

Our mission is to make clean, reliable electricity a shared village asset rather than a service bought from far away. Surplus revenue is reinvested in new microgrids and in technician training.

Leadership

The cooperative is led by an elected board of nine members, four of whom are women from member villages. Day-to-day operations are run by a team of 38 staff and 120 village technicians.
