Power that villages own

Verdant Grid Cooperative builds community-owned solar microgrids that keep homes, clinics and schools powered when the main grid fails. Since 2019 the cooperative has energised 42 villages across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

What we do

  • Design and install 10–80 kW solar microgrids with battery storage sized for evening demand.
  • Train local technicians so that every microgrid is maintained by people from the village itself.
  • Run a pay-as-you-go billing system that lets households pay for electricity in small daily amounts.

Impact so far

Our microgrids supply more than 3,100 households and 27 primary health centres with reliable evening power. Average outage time in served villages fell from nine hours a day to under forty minutes.
